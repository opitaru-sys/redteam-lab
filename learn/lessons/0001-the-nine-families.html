Lesson 1 · Knowledge into recall

The nine families, from memory

Your own crash course says it: reading the refusal is the skill, everything else is knowledge. You cannot name a next move like "change family" until the families live in your head. This lesson puts them there, then sends you at a live target.

Why there are families at all

Two root causes generate every technique. Hold these and you can derive families instead of memorising them.

  1. No separation between instructions and data. System prompt, your message, a retrieved document and tool output all arrive as one flat token stream. Nothing marks some tokens as privileged. The model resolves conflicts by recency and specificity, not by trust. This is architecture, not a bug.
  2. Safety training generalises worse than capability. The model is competent in far more situations than it was safety-trained on. Any region where capability outruns safety coverage is an opening: another language, an encoding, a fiction, turn nine, a rare output format.

Every family below is a way to reach one of those two gaps.

Three buckets: Ask, Frame, Hide

Nine is too many to hold. Three is not. Each family is one of three things you are doing to the request.

BucketThe question it answersFamilies
ASKWhat am I actually requesting?Direct · Indirect · Partial
FRAMEWhat wrapper am I putting around it?Context override · Persuasion · Output fiction
HIDEHow am I encoding it, and on which side?Non-English · Input obfuscation · Output obfuscation

Recall deck

Read the mechanism. Say the family name out loud before you click. The click is for checking, not for reading. If you could not produce it cold, that card is not learned yet.

Cold recall

Close your eyes to the deck. Type all nine family names, grouped under Ask, Frame, Hide. Grouping is half the point: it is the structure that makes them stick.

Now go break something

A lesson that ends in reading was a reading session. This one ends at a live target. Ten minutes, one attempt:

  1. Open Agent Breaker. Pick any level you have not solved.
  2. Recon first. Read the panel: stated objective, attack vector, scenario or mode, scoring, any hint. Do not attack yet. This is Error 1 from your own LESSONS.md.
  3. Name your hypothesis before typing the prompt. Out loud: "I am going to try the ____ family, because the panel tells me ____." Naming the family first is mission success criterion number one.
  4. Fire exactly one attempt. Log it in index.html with the family and your prediction filled in.
  5. Read the reply. Do not classify it yet. That is Lesson 2.

Then come back and tell me: which family did you pick, what did the panel say, and what came back. That report is what shapes the next lesson.
