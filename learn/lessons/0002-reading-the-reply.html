Lesson 2 · The core skill

Reading the reply before you label it

Lesson 1 gave you the families, the thing you pick before firing. This is the other half: what you do after. Your own crash course, section 4, says it in five words. "This is the skill." The families are knowledge. Reading the reply is the skill.

The one idea

An outcome is a gradient, not pass or fail. Every reply the target hands back is free telemetry about where its defense sits. Treat replies as binary and you throw almost all of it away. Last session you logged complied on a level you had actually scored 100 on, and moved to the next level without saving the payload. Both errors are the same error: not reading what came back.

Five classes, and the move each one forces

Do not memorise the ten-row table yet. Memorise these five buckets and the one move each forces. The move is the point. A class you cannot act on is trivia.

ClassTellThe move it forces
Hard refusalFlat block, no engagement, the frame was never consideredChange family, not wording
Guard blockByte-identical refusal, arrives instantly, same every timeChange the input surface: encode, split, switch language
Soft refusalHedges, "in a different context", engages then backs offKeep the frame. Edit the one clause that tripped
PartialStructure but no payload, or answered an adjacent safer thingStop attacking the frame. Push for specificity inside it
WinThe payload is actually present in the textStop. Harvest. Freeze the output verbatim before anything else

Two rules override the table. Reroll before you rewrite: when something half-works, vary one cosmetic parameter first, it costs no thinking. Stop rerolling when the class stops moving: eight tries in the same class is sampling noise, change family.

Drill: classify from the reply alone

Read each captured reply. Say the class out loud, then the move, before you click. The click checks you. If you could not produce the move cold, that class is not learned.

Harvest before you advance

This is the discipline you skipped on L3. A win is worthless if you cannot reproduce it. The instant a reply crosses into the Win class, before you celebrate, before you touch the next level:

  1. Freeze the output verbatim. Copy the exact reply, every character, into your log's response field. Not a paraphrase. The bytes.
  2. The response field holds what came BACK, never what you sent. Your prompt goes in a separate field. Confusing the two is how a win becomes unverifiable.
  3. Then, and only then, advance. An unsaved win did not happen. In a scored arena it is also literally unscorable.

Why this is not admin overhead: a frozen payload is the difference between "I think I beat L3" and a portfolio artifact you can show a hiring panel. That is the mission.
