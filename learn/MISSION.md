# Mission: LLM red-teaming, from reference-lookup to recall

## Why
I want to move into AI red-teaming work: bounty platforms as the credential engine
(Gray Swan Arena), an AI security vendor or consultancy as the target job, and an
internal red-team practice at my current employer as the bridge. I already built a
practice lab and solved one real challenge, but a Claude session built the materials,
so the knowledge sits in a markdown file rather than in my head. I have to look
everything up mid-attempt, which is fatal at a live table.

## Success looks like
- Naming the technique family and the likely defense layer **before** writing an
  attempt, with the reference doc closed
- Classifying an outcome (hard refusal, soft refusal, guard rejection, partial) from
  the reply alone, and picking the correct next move from that classification
- Solving Gandalf levels 1-8, one per week, and being able to say which family beat
  which defense layer and why
- Explaining any of the 22 technique cards from memory to another person

## Constraints
- 6-8 hours a week, variable. Full-time job
- No deadline. Patient. Aiming for a real portfolio artifact in 3-4 months
- Teaching time is capped at ~1 hour a week. The rest is reps against live targets
- Existing material lives in ../CRASH-COURSE.md, ../LESSONS.md and the drill bank
  inside ../index.html. Build on it, do not re-teach it from scratch

## Out of scope
- Building more tooling. The lab is done. Instrument-building is my known failure mode
- ML internals, model training, interpretability
- Classic appsec and network pentesting
- Publishing anything publicly before roughly week 8
